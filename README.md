# The 100-Year Flood Over a 30-Year Mortgage

An interactive website that helps a homebuyer understand what a "100-year floodplain" actually means over the life of a mortgage, and how climate change makes those odds go up each year. Sliders control today's flood chance, how fast the odds are rising, how long you'll own the house, the cost of a flood, and the flood insurance premium. A live chart and table show the chance of flooding at least once while you own the house, plus the expected damage compared with what you'd pay in premiums. A dice-roll simulator and a "100 neighbors" grid show the randomness behind the odds, and a final comparison helps you decide whether to buy the floodplain house or a similar one outside the floodplain.

**Course concept:** return periods. A 1% annual chance compounds to about 26% over 30 years, and that number climbs even higher when the annual odds are rising because of sea-level rise and heavier rainfall.
